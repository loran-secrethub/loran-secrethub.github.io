Ice Cream Petshop

A pixel-style training game that teaches new shop staff which ice cream flavors suit each pet's health needs.

See how it plays

Playable version coming soon

The scenario

Your first day at the shop

New staff need to remember six flavors and what each one does for a pet. A list is easy to forget. So instead of reading one, they serve real customers, and every customer needs the right scoop.

"Welcome to my presence! I am Princess Chloe. My Mama says that even at 11 years old, my sassiness remains unmatched. I may move with a more graceful, slow pace now, but my health is a top priority in this kingdom! And remember, it simply must be pink!"

Customer no. 1

Her order: Strawberry Sniffer. It's pink, and its immune boost keeps her healthy.

How it plays

Learn, listen, guess

  1. 1Meet Eli

    Eli, the shop manager, walks new staff through the six pet-safe flavors and what each one does.

  2. 2Read the clue

    Six pets come in, one at a time. The way each pet describes itself is a clue to what it needs.

  3. ♥♥♥♥♥ 5 tries per pet

    3Guess the order

    Pick letters one by one to spell the right flavor, like Hangman. Each wrong letter costs one of five tries.

Under the hood

How I built it

Everything runs on Storyline triggers and variables.

  • Counting tries

    A variable keeps track of how many tries are left for each pet, and goes down by one with every wrong letter.

  • Checking letters

    When a letter is picked, triggers check whether it's in the flavor name, and show it in the right spot if it is.

  • Six customers

    The game remembers which pet you're serving, so it knows which customer comes next.
