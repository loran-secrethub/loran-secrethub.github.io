Pop the Verbs

A bubble-popping game built with Phaser. Every bubble holds a word, and learners find and pop the 15 verbs hiding among the other words.

By Loran Iñosa, a former ESL and preschool teacher who now builds e-learning. Read my journey

Playable version coming soon

Learning goal
Tell verbs apart from other words
Win condition
Pop 15 verbs
Built with
Phaser · JavaScript
Type
Educational game

How it plays

  1. 01

    Bubbles appear

    Each bubble holds a word. Some are verbs, and some aren't.

  2. 02

    Pop the verbs

    The learner clicks the bubbles they think hold verbs, and avoids the others.

  3. 03

    Reach 15

    Popping 15 verbs completes the game.

The learning design

The learning objective came first: learners identify verbs among a mix of words. Instead of underlining verbs on a worksheet, they find them in a game, and every pop is a small decision: is this word a verb?

Teacher first

The learning goal comes before the technology.
