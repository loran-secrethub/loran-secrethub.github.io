Pop the Verbs

A bubble-popping game where players find the verbs hiding among other words.

The idea

Why a game?

Spotting verbs on a worksheet is quiet and slow. In this game, every pop is a quick decision, and a sound tells the player right away whether they got it right.

Every pop asks

Is this a verb?

How it plays

Start, pop, score

  1. 1Start

    The main menu plays music and has a How to Play popup, so players know the rules before they begin.

  2. 2Pop the verbs

    30 word bubbles float and bounce around: 15 verbs and 15 other words. Players get 15 pops. Right and wrong pops play different sounds.

  3. 3See the score

    After 15 pops, the round ends and shows how many verbs the player found, like "12 / 15 verbs found."

Under the hood

How I built it

The game is split into four screens, each with one job:

  1. Bootloads the font
  2. Preloaderpictures and sounds
  3. Main menumusic, how to play
  4. Gameplay and score

  • Bubbles that bounce

    Each bubble starts moving in a random direction and bounces off the edges of the screen, so it never floats away.

  • Words that follow

    Many times a second, the game moves each word to the center of its bubble, so they always travel together.

  • Clean pops

    When a bubble is popped, the bubble and its word are removed right away, so the game stays smooth.
