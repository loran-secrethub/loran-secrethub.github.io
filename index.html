Internal tool · In team use

Worktext Studio

A browser tool that lets my team preview, check and review worktext pages before they're rendered to PDF.

  • Simulates the PDF renderer and flags pages that overflow
  • Sticky-note reviews that stay attached to edited text
  • Shared comments with Google Sign-In, stored in a Google Sheet through Apps Script
